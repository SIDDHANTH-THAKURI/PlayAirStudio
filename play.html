How to play

Fretting hand (chords side of the frame)

  • Chord grid — your index fingertip is the cursor. A cell only takes over while you're actually pointing (☝️ index out, other fingers in), so drifting across the wall with a relaxed hand changes nothing.
  • Sign chords — five chords bound to ✋ ✌️ 🤘 👌 🤙. Where your hand is stops mattering entirely.
  • Deaden: ✌️ index + middle in grid mode, ✊ fist in sign mode — plucks turn into percussive chks.
  • Vibrato: 🤘 index + little finger, then shake up and down. In sign mode just shake — nothing else is listening to your position.
  • Bend: pinch thumb + index (middle finger out) for up to a whole step. Chord-grid mode only — in sign mode that exact hand is 👌, so it picks a chord instead.

Plucking hand (no fast moves needed)

  • Fingerstyle — the number of open digits picks one string: 1 → 1st (high e), 2 → 2nd (B), 3 → 3rd (G), 4 → 4th (D), 5 → 5th (A), little finger alone → 6th (low E). Each shape sounds once; go back toward a fist, or move to a different count, to fire again.
  • Strumming — hold ✊ ✌️ 🤘 🤙 👌 for about half a second to run that sign's pattern for as long as you hold it. ✋ open palm stops. Patterns pick up chord changes live.
  • Too slow? The Tempo slider (or [ ]) moves the whole song, live and without stuttering. To speed up just one pattern, set its feel to 1½× or 2× in the strum maker — the song stays put and that strum gets busier.
  • Move toward the edge of frame to brighten the tone (bridge pickup); toward the middle for warmth.

Switching modes (same ritual on both hands)

  • Hold 👍 thumbs-up for one second — a two-option dial opens around that hand.
  • Tilt the hand left or right to move the highlight.
  • Open your palm to accept. Drop the shape and the dial closes without changing anything.
  • The left hand's dial switches chord grid ⇄ sign chords; the right hand's switches fingerstyle ⇄ strumming. Both are also plain buttons in the panel.
